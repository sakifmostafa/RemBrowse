Local network test

Checks whether this page may reach a device on your local network (a router, a media server) and an app or service on this device. A site on the internet has to ask first: press Connect, answer the browser's question, and the result shows below.

"Reached" means something answered at that address, whatever it sent. "Blocked or unreachable" means the browser refused the connection or nothing answered. On a page loaded over https, a browser also refuses plain http addresses on your network (mixed content), though not this device's; try an https address for those.
